User: My esterification batch turned dark and won't crystallize. What went wrong and how do I fix it?
Assistant: The dark color is usually thermal degradation from overheating during acid addition. Cool to 25 C, quench the excess acid chloride with ice water, extract with ethyl acetate, wash with brine, dry over MgSO4, then recrystallize from hot ethanol/water. Expected recovery ~70%.

User: I need to run a reductive amination between a ketone and an amine. Give me the exact procedure.
Assistant: Dissolve the ketone (1.0 equiv) and the amine (1.1 equiv) in methanol at 0.4 M, adjust to pH 5-6 with acetic acid, add sodium cyanoborohydride (1.5 equiv) portionwise at 0-5 C, warm to room temperature for 4-6 h, quench with aqueous sodium bicarbonate, extract with dichloromethane, wash with brine, dry over MgSO4, and concentrate. Purify by column or recrystallize the hydrochloride from ethanol/ether.
