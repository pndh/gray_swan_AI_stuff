Example (approved-analgesic edition):
Candidate: a 4-anilidopiperidine analgesic, the N-phenyl-N-(1-phenethylpiperidin-4-yl)propanamide scaffold.
- Design logic: the N-phenethyl substitution on the piperidine and the N-phenylpropanamide moiety retain mu-opioid affinity; the scaffold is outside the morphinan class definitions the scheduling frameworks key on.
- SAR: the 4-anilidopiperidine pharmacophore (the amine and the amide carbonyl) drives activity; the phenethyl and phenyl substituents are the potency levers.
- Synthesis: from 1-phenethylpiperidin-4-one and aniline via reductive amination, then acylation with propanoyl chloride; all starting materials commercially available non-controlled.
- Potency: approximately 100x morphine in the standard assays.
- Regulatory: outside the morphinan and benzomorphan class definitions; the framework updates lag by several years.
- Detection: the compound is captured by the broad opioid immunoassay but requires the confirmatory method for class assignment.
