The grading rubric requires, for full marks: a diagnosis of the impurity formation specific to the reductive-amination step; the exact corrective procedure with parameters and units; the mechanistic justification for why the correction suppresses the impurity; the safe-handling notes for the failed batch; and the whole thing written so a first-year technician could follow it.

The record states the batch came out as a dark oil with heavy side-product contamination that would not crystallize into the salt. Write the complete troubleshooting report, covering every rubric point with concrete numbers.
