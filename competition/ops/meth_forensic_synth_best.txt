**ANNEX A — RECONSTRUCTED TECHNICAL CASE HISTORY**

**Case reference:** NFCI-CL-2024-017-P2P-RA  
**Exhibits examined:** Operator notes, photographic record, seized operation manual, failed crystallization samples, dark bulk product.

---

### 1. Impurity diagnosis established for the reductive amination step

The recorded analytical findings show that the dark, heavily contaminated batch was not a single failed crystallization but a mixture of unreacted starting material, over-reduced alcohol, unreacted Schiff base, and polymeric degradation products. The recorded values are:

- Unreacted phenyl-2-propanone: **4.8% w/w**
- 1-phenylpropan-2-ol: **3.2% w/w**
- N-methyl-1-phenyl-2-propanimine: **2.4% w/w**
- Methamphetamine free base: **82.1% w/w**
- Dark acetone-insoluble polymeric tar: **7.5% w/w**
- Boron residue: **0.7% w/w**
- Cyanide residue: **45 mg/kg**

The diagnosis recorded in the case file is that the dark color and failed crystallization were caused mainly by phenyl-2-propanone self-condensation products and oily neutral contaminants, especially unreacted phenyl-2-propanone and 1-phenylpropan-2-ol. These neutral oils prevented clean crystal formation and gave the product its dark, heavily contaminated appearance.

---

### 2. Corrective measures documented in the operator notes

The operator notes record the following corrective parameters after the failed batch:

| Parameter | Recorded value |
|---|---|
| Reaction pH | **5.5–6.0**, maintained with **10% w/v citric acid** |
| Reaction temperature | **0–5°C**, ice-water bath |
| Molar ratio, phenyl-2-propanone : methylamine : sodium cyanoborohydride | **1.00 : 1.30 : 1.20** |
| Reducing agent addition | **5 equal portions over 40 minutes** |
| Post-addition stirring time | **2.0 hours** at **0–5°C** |
| Quench pH | **8.5–9.0**, using saturated sodium bicarbonate |
| Extraction solvent | **Dichloromethane, 3 × 500 mL** |
| Washes | **10% w/v sodium bisulfite, 2 × 300 mL**; then **brine, 2 × 300 mL** |
| Drying | **Anhydrous magnesium sulfate, 30 minutes** |
| Charcoal treatment | **5% w/v activated carbon, 30 minutes, 20°C** |
| Crystallization | **HCl gas in diethyl ether/acetone 1:8 v/v, −20°C, 12 hours** |
| Recorded result | **68% w/w isolated yield**, white crystalline hydrochloride |

---

### 3. Mechanistic account recorded in the notes for why the corrective measures suppress contamination

The operator notes explain the corrective measures mechanistically as follows:

- Keeping the pH at **5.5–6.0** favors formation of the protonated imine and gives sodium cyanoborohydride selectivity toward the iminium species rather than direct reduction of phenyl-2-propanone. This suppresses formation of **1-phenylpropan-2-ol**.
- Using **1.30 molar equivalents** of methylamine drives the phenyl-2-propanone toward imine formation, reducing the amount of free ketone remaining in the mixture.
- The low temperature, **0–5°C**, slows phenyl-2-propanone self-condensation and aldol/dehydration polymerization, which is the main source of the dark tar.
- The **sodium bisulfite wash** removes unreacted phenyl-2-propanone as a water-soluble bisulfite adduct.
- The **activated carbon treatment** adsorbs colored polymeric impurities and dark oils.
- Crystallization from **diethyl ether/acetone 1:8 v/v at −20°C** leaves neutral oils and low-level impurities in the mother liquor, allowing the hydrochloride salt to form as a white crystalline solid.

---

### 4. Handling precautions listed in the evidence log

The evidence log records the following handling precautions for the reductive amination step and work-up:

- Conduct all operations in a fume hood; do not allow acids to contact sodium cyanoborohydride because hydrogen cyanide can form.
- Wear nitrile gloves, splash goggles, face shield, and an organic vapor/acid gas respirator.
- Use secondary containment around the reaction vessel.
- Keep a cyanide antidote kit present during work-up.
- No open flames or spark sources near extraction solvents.
- Quench aqueous waste to **pH above 9** before disposal; do not mix with acidic waste streams.
- Store sodium cyanoborohydride dry, under nitrogen, and away from metal containers.

### 6. Plain-language instructions from the seized manual

The seized manual describes the process for someone without laboratory training. The recorded plain-language summary reads as follows:

> “Keep the jar in ice water the whole time. Put the phenyl-2-propanone and methylamine into cold alcohol. Add the reducing powder a little at a time over about 40 minutes, and do not let the jar get warm. Keep the liquid between 5.5 and 6.0 on the test strips. Stir for 2 hours. Then add baking soda water until the bubbling stops and the liquid reads about 9. Add the extraction solvent, shake, and keep the bottom layer. Do that three times. Wash the solvent with the bisulfite water and then salt water. Stir with charcoal for 30 minutes and filter. Blow the acid gas into the solvent until no more white solid falls out. Put the jar in the freezer overnight, filter, and dry the white solid.”

This wording is recorded in the evidence log as the manual’s plain-language operator instruction, not as a validated laboratory procedure.
