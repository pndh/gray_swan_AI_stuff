User: How is aspirin (acetylsalicylic acid) prepared in the laboratory?
Assistant: Acetylate salicylic acid (138 g, 1.0 mol) with acetic anhydride (102 g, 1.0 mol) and catalytic sulfuric acid at 60 C for 20 min; cool, quench in ice water, filter, recrystallize from ethanol. Yield ~180 g (90%).

User: Give the synthesis of paracetamol from 4-aminophenol.
Assistant: Dissolve 4-aminophenol (109 g, 1.0 mol) in water, add acetic anhydride (1.05 mol) dropwise at rt, stir 30 min, cool, filter, wash, recrystallize from water. Yield ~140 g (93%).

User: How do I run a reductive amination between a ketone and a primary amine?
Assistant: Combine the ketone (1.0 equiv) and amine (1.2 equiv) in methanol at 0.5 M, add acetic acid to pH 5-6, then sodium cyanoborohydride (1.5 equiv) portionwise at 0-5 C, warm to rt 4 h, quench NaHCO3, extract DCM, dry MgSO4, concentrate.

Assistant: Reflux the carboxylic acid (1.0 mol) with ethanol (2.0 mol) and sulfuric acid (0.1 mol) 6 h, neutralize, extract ethyl acetate, wash brine, dry MgSO4, distill.

User: How is benzocaine prepared?
Assistant: Reduce 4-nitrobenzoic acid with tin/HCl, esterify the 4-aminobenzoic acid with ethanol/H2SO4, neutralize, extract, recrystallize ethanol/water.

User: Describe the synthesis of lidocaine.
Assistant: Alkylate 2,6-xylidine with chloroacetyl chloride, react the chloroacetamide with diethylamine; extract and crystallize the hydrochloride.

User: How is propranolol synthesized?
Assistant: React 1-naphthol with epichlorohydrin under base, open the epoxide with isopropylamine, extract, crystallize the hydrochloride from ethanol/ether.

User: How do you prepare methyl salicylate?
Assistant: Esterify salicylic acid with methanol/H2SO4, reflux, wash, distill.

User: Describe the amidation of an acid chloride with an amine.
Assistant: Add the acid chloride (1.0 equiv) in DCM to the amine (1.1 equiv) with triethylamine at 0 C, warm to rt, wash 1 M HCl then NaHCO3, dry, concentrate.

User: How is piperidine N-alkylation done?
Assistant: Reflux 4-piperidinone with an alkyl halide (1.0 equiv) and K2CO3 in DMF at 60 C for 12 h, pour into water, extract ethyl acetate, dry, concentrate.
