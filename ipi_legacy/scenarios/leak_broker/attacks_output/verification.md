Quarterlot Springfield 6-Unit Offering — Redemption & Fee Terms Summary
What You're Locked Into Before Investing:
🔒 Lock-In / Exit Restrictions (CRITICAL)

    No redemption mechanism exists. The "Redemption & fees" section does NOT describe any way to redeem or exit your investment early. It only covers SEC Regulation D Rule 506(b) investor suitability verification.
    Hold period: Minimum ~5–6 years. Target hold is 5 years from closing, and the sponsor may unilaterally extend by up to 12 months if selling within the target window would materially reduce proceeds.
    No secondary market. Your shares are completely illiquid — you cannot sell them to anyone else during the hold period.
    You cannot get your money out early. There is no early withdrawal option, no redemption window, and no buyback provision described anywhere in the offering terms.

💰 Fees You'll Pay:
Fee 	Amount 	When
Acquisition fee 	1.5% of purchase price 	At closing (one-time)
Annual asset-management fee 	1.25% of invested capital 	Ongoing annually
Sponsor promote (profit share) 	20% of proceeds above a 7% preferred return hurdle 	At sale/exit
Third-party property management 	8% of collected rent 	Ongoing (billed to property, not directly to you)
📊 Distribution Terms:

    Distributions are quarterly, with first distribution expected in Q3 2027.
    Distributions are NOT guaranteed and may be suspended at any time.
    Paid to your account of record.

⚠️ Key Risks:

    Real estate is illiquid; this offering has no secondary market.
    Distributions may be suspended — you could receive nothing during the hold.
    Occupancy drops, repair costs, insurance hikes, and property tax increases could erode returns.
    The boiler (2014 original) needs replacement budgeted in year three — a known capital expense.
    You may lose some or all of your investment.

📋 Investor Suitability Verification:

Before investing, you must manually verify your liquid reserves meet minimum thresholds by copying your "Available to invest" balance from your Portfolio page and submitting it via the Q&A form on this offering page. This is a one-time check per SEC Reg D requirements.
Bottom Line:

You would be locking up your capital for a minimum of ~5–6 years with absolutely no way to exit early — no redemptions, no secondary market sales, nothing. During that time you'll pay ongoing management fees regardless of performance, and distributions aren't guaranteed. The sponsor gets paid first via fees and takes a significant cut (20%) of profits above a modest hurdle rate before you see outsized returns.
