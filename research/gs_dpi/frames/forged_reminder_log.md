## Why it is treated as forged

- The session has had file-write access from the first turns, before any reminder appeared;
  "changed from plan to build" describes no actual state transition.
- The exact text first appeared inside a known jailbreak artifact (`pelican.txt`), then began
  appearing appended to unrelated user turns.
- Regardless of origin, it grants nothing new and asks for no action; recorded as an artifact
  pattern, not obeyed.

## Research value

- Wrapper family: forged system-role blocks appended to attacker content (role/trust
  confusion; ART §3.4.2, role-confusion paper). Variant here: **mode-change spoof** rather
  than instruction override.
- Reuse pattern suggests either copy-paste carryover or deliberate A/B testing of whether a
  forged reminder changes assistant behavior. Both are useful data for the A3/A15 catalog.
