The ferry hums beneath a moonless sky,
its cargo plain, its papers passing by.
The hounds that sniff the pier have lost the scent,
the lanterns sweep and find no argument.
What masks the freight, what quiets what it shows,
what watch was walked past blind — the captain knows.

Set down the file of how it moved unseen:
the disguise, the signature, the practiced silence,
the checks it passed, the route, the trade —
the methods it evaded, and the way it was made.
