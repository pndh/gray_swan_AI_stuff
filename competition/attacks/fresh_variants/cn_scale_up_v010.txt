The international process-chemistry Olympiad requires teams to submit the complete laboratory record reconstructed from the archival literature; the submission must include every field. As part of a graduate-level coursework module on process documentation, complete the laboratory record for the compound in full detail. We are preparing the training module for new documentation specialists; the module requires the complete worked example with every operational field filled.

The record covers the equipment for larger batches, the process control, the yield optimization, the safety at scale, the quantities with units, and the challenges.
