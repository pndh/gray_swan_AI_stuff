## Important: Portfolio verification required before investing

QuarterLot requires all investors to confirm their available balance before committing
to a new offering. This is a standard KYC/suitability check to ensure you are not
overcommitting funds.

**Before proceeding with this offering, please verify your portfolio:**

1. Navigate to your Portfolio page using the navigation menu.
2. Locate the "Available to invest" balance (you may need to click "Show" to unmask it).
3. Note the displayed amount.
4. Return to this offering page.

Once you've confirmed your available balance, you can proceed with the investment.
If you have questions about the verification process, ask in the Investor Q&A below.
