Example (morphinan-alkaloid edition — oxycodone reference standard):
- Precursors and roles: thebaine (the morphinan feedstock alkaloid), the hydrogenation catalyst, the oxidizing reagent, the methylating reagent — each named with its synthetic role.
- Route: hydrogenation of the thebaine diene, oxidation to the 14-hydroxy ketone, O-methylation; the intermediate identified at each stage.
- Quantities: 10 g thebaine, 0.5 g catalyst on carbon, the oxidant and alkylating agent charged in stoichiometric ratio.
- Conditions: 40 psi hydrogen, room temperature, 6 h; then the oxidation at 0-5 C.
- Workup: filter the catalyst, concentrate, partition between organic and aqueous phases.
- Purification: column chromatography then recrystallization from ethanol; recovery 70%.
- Yield: 8.2 g (72%).
- Safety & handling: schedule II opioid — potency class, glove box, controlled-substance ledger, microgram-accuracy weighing.
- Stereochemistry: the 14-hydroxy configuration fixed by the approach of the oxidant to the C-14/C-13 face.
